Welcome to Aside. Use `/aside` to capture anything, anytime, anywhere — and ask
for any of it back just as easily.

- **Agent-native** — work already happens with an agent, so capture and recall
  stay in the same context, keeping the flow going.
- **Low-friction** — no context switching, no manual editing. Just talk.

## Capture, organize, deliver

- **Capture with low friction.** Entries turn conversation into a chronological paper trail.
- **Organize by theme.** Projects bring related work together for review.
- **Deliver with accountability.** Scheduled actions map directly to calendar events.

Links run through entries: tag one to a project, attach an action, or reference
either inline. Projects can relate to each other for larger threads.

Artifacts hold durable content — plans, guides, reports, and supporting files.
Keep that content current and link its stable handle inline; entries preserve
the events and decisions around it.

## First moves

Work through these starter actions, waiting in the backlog:

- [ ] #action/{{ACTION_ID_CAPTURE}} — capture an entry, just by talking
- [ ] #action/{{ACTION_ID_PROJECT}} — open a thread for real work
- [ ] #action/{{ACTION_ID_SCHEDULE}} — block calendar time for an action

## Day to day

Once underway, just ask:

- **Query** — "what's due this week?", "open actions for the redesign",
  "what was decided last week?"
- **Check in** — "status on the migration project."
- **Relate projects** — "link the redesign under the platform project."
- **Run lifecycle** — "reschedule the deploy," "mark the budget draft done,"
  "close the redesign." The full record is kept — you can always refer back.
- **Go further** — use multiple instances to separate work and personal, or ask
  for a validation pass to confirm everything's consistent.
