# Name: Aside

## The Aside

In theater, an aside is when a character turns to the audience and says what's actually true — brief, honest, more real than the main performance. In conversation, "as an aside..." is something said in passing that turns out to be the most important thing said.

An aside is the meeting that changed your mind. The research rabbit hole that surfaced an insight. The decision made over coffee. The plan sketched on the back of a thought. The half-formed idea you almost didn't say out loud. Everything worth remembering starts as an aside.

> "Life moves pretty fast. If you don't stop and look around once in a while, you could miss it." — Ferris Bueller

## Purpose and point of view

The project starts from a question: as AI shows its power, how do people
rediscover what makes human life human? Society has long relied on people to
fulfill productive, institutional, and utilitarian roles. The hope is that AI
can shoulder more of those demands, freeing people to redirect their attention
toward a more artistic, poetic, inspiring, and hopeful life.

With Aside, people invite AI to empower the life they want to lead. It supports
accumulating inspiration and delegating mundane work. The person decides what
makes their life meaningful; AI handles the execution. The tool offers
simplicity and clarity that a free mind can appreciate.

The artistic name is deliberate. It evokes this poetic ambition and gives the
tool a supporting role alongside the life its user chooses to lead.

> Let AI be useful; make your life beautiful.

## How It Maps to the System

The core interaction is literally an aside — you briefly branch off within your agent conversation, say something, then carry on. Low friction, no context switching. The system sits alongside your work, not in front of it. What you say is captured honestly, in your voice. It becomes part of a lasting record you can return to.

## In Use

- "I logged it in Aside"
- "Check my Aside for that week"
- "What does Aside show for that project?"

The name works as the system, the agent, and the record. You talk to Aside, Aside remembers.

## Why Not Other Names

The name was chosen over alternatives because it:

- **Doesn't over-describe.** It doesn't say "notes" or "productivity" or "AI." It leaves space for people to discover what it does.
- **Carries positive energy.** Asides in theater are moments of clarity and wit. The word has no negative connotation.
- **Matches the interaction exactly.** You don't need a metaphor to explain it — the system interaction IS an aside.
- **Is common enough to understand, uncommon enough to own.** Everyone knows the word; nobody uses it as a product name.
- **Works grammatically.** As a noun ("my Aside"), as a reference ("in Aside"), as a concept ("make an aside"). Doesn't need to verb.
